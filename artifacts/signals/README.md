# Signals

A signal is a structured observation that may affect product, engineering,
growth or operations.

Use a signal when the issue is recurring, strategically important, or not yet a
single clear task.

## Template

```md
---
kind: signal
status: open
priority: medium
created_at: YYYY-MM-DD
frequency: 1
sources: []
tags: []
---

# Title

## Observation

## Evidence

## Possible Causes

## Suggested Next Action

## Timeline
```
